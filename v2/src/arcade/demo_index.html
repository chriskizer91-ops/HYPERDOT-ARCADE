Intro tests

Three new ways into the arcade, plus the one that's live now. In every version the camera stays on the table through the glitch, and the arcade's games pass slowly enough to watch. The landing and the reveal are the same in all of them.

  1. AWhirlpoolThe table twists into a whirlpool round the frozen ball and spins you down the drain, then you sink slowly through a ring of the games.Watchthe moment the table starts to turn
  2. BInto the displayYou're pulled face first into the playfield. It breaks into bigger and bigger LED dots until you pass between them and sink down a shaft lined with video walls of the games.Watchthe dots resolving into the walls of screens
  3. CSwallowedThe machine convulses and bursts. You shrink with it and fall in slow motion among giant bumpers, flippers and slabs of the playfield, the games floating round you, until gravity snaps back.Watchthe burst, and the slow fall among the parts
