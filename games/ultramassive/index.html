ULTRAMASSIVE

A 3D pinball campaign. Light phenomena on the Core to open the Gateway, shoot the black hole, and fall into a boss world. Clear all three and the Ultramassive wakes.

  1. The Core is Supermassive's table: 6 flippers, 3 playfields, 8 ramps. Every phenomenon (vortex, starfall, multiball, eclipse, quasar, lensing, neutron star, pulsar, railgun, binary merger) charges the Gateway. Two charges open it.
  2. When the Gateway is open, the black hole's magnet catches the ball and drops it into the next world.
  3. The Devourer: break its five armour plates, then hit its body. Stay away from its maw when it inhales, and smash the meteors it spits.
  4. The Pulsar Warden: shoot through the gaps in its spinning shield to hit the core. Its beams zap the ball; its EMP throws it outward.
  5. The Twins: hit one, then the other within 4 seconds, or the damage heals. Dive into an open horizon for a wormhole jump. At the end they merge.
  6. In every world: ramps fire light-lances at the boss, loops charge a triple-damage super strike, and the drop targets in front of the boss stun it for double damage. At half health it rages and you get a second ball.
  7. Lose the ball in a world and the next ball starts back at the Core; the boss keeps its wounds. Beat the boss and the ball comes home through the white hole, one depth deeper.
  8. The Ultramassive: break the three relics that orbit it, then hit the core. Ramps, loops, the wormhole and the railgun all fire lances. Win and the Big Bang starts a new universe with every score multiplied.
