SUPERMASSIVE

One big table falling into a black hole: 6 flippers, 3 playfields, 8 ramps and wireforms, and about 100 things to hit. Start phenomena to fall deeper. Light 6 of the 10 and feed the black hole.

  1. Each flipper button works three flippers. Left: lower left, upper left, and the Upper Deck flipper. Right: lower right, upper right, and the Orbital Station flipper.
  2. Skill shots. A soft plunge drops into the top lanes: the flashing lane scores 100,000. Hold the left flipper while you plunge and the skill ramp carries the ball up to the Upper Deck; shoot the Skybridge from there within 8 seconds for 250,000.
  3. The Orbital Station is the raised playfield on the left. The left ramp docks there, and so do the right ramp's diverter and the Skybridge; the station flipper shoots its saucers and the O-R-B targets, which count as a docking (and relight the jackpot in multiball). Every 2 dockings, or the Q-U-A targets, light the lock: put 2 balls in the station saucers for 3-ball multiball. The gold jackpot target is on the station; after a jackpot the right ramp scores the super jackpot.
  4. The Upper Deck on the right is where the right ramp lands. Two binary black holes orbit each other on it: a slow ball is swallowed and thrown out of the twin. S-A-R targets along the top, lens targets on the right, and the Skybridge flies from its top corner over the table to the station.
  5. The black hole in the middle is a turntable with 4 accretion pegs, 4 orbiting moons and real gravity. Time slows near it, and every score doubles while it does. When its ring is lit, its magnet captures the ball.
  6. Top lanes A-B-Y-S-S raise the bonus multiplier, relight the kickback and raise the save post. In- and outlanes W-A-R-P arm the mag-save in the right outlane. The flipper buttons move the lit lanes. Each inlane doubles the opposite ramp for 4 seconds.
  7. Up one orbit and down the other is a loop. Loops and ramps chain into combos. Every 3rd loop lights video mode at the wormhole scoop, which also sends the ball up to the deck.
  8. Hit the captive comet into its target 3 times to light an extra ball at the wormhole. Every so often a second comet pops up and sweeps across the centre: hit it for a Comet Strike and 2,000 a turn on the centre spinner.
  9. The railgun hole in the lower right aims at the left orbit, the top lanes and the right orbit in turn. Fire with the plunger, or with either flipper when it's your only ball. Hit the flashing target for a bullseye.
  10. The neutron star is a heavy ball on a spring track behind the right ramp: push it to the top. The vari-target on the right wall scores more the harder you hit it.

PHENOMENA

  1. Vortex: the 3 right drop targets. The black hole spins up and its magnet lights; everything scores 2× for 20 seconds.
  2. Starfall: the 5 left drop targets. All 6 star rollovers light for 30 seconds; collect all 6 for a supernova.
  3. Multiball: lock 2 balls at the station.
  4. Eclipse: hit all 4 moons. The black hole lights; a capture rides the spiral wireform for 150,000.
  5. Quasar: Q-U-A under the station and S-A-R on the deck (a loop spots a letter). Jets, orbits and spinners score 3× for 30 seconds.
  6. Lensing: the 3 lens targets on the deck. Ramps score 2× and the magnetar field between the slingshots bends the ball for 30 seconds.
  7. Neutron star: push it to the top of its track twice.
  8. Pulsar: the neutron star's beams sweep the table. Hit the pulsar target on the left while a beam is on it, twice.
  9. Railgun: 2 bullseyes.
  10. Binary merger: 3 jumps through the binary black holes.
  11. Every 2 phenomena you fall one depth deeper: a new backglass, new music, stronger gravity and bigger awards. Light 6 and SUPERMASSIVE is lit: shoot the black hole for 6 balls at 5× scoring for 45 seconds. Survive it and the Big Bang starts a new universe, every score multiplied by its number.
  12. Beat a high score to enter your initials with the flippers and plunger. Every game ends with a match; replays and matches add credits.
